Install and update

Keep the app current

  1. Tap DownloadTap the green Download CropCare AI button above.
  2. Open the fileWhen the download finishes, tap CropCareAI-release.apk.
  3. Allow installationIf Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. Then return to the downloaded file.
  4. Install or updateTap Install. If CropCare AI is already on your phone, tap Update instead. Your saved app data will remain.
  5. Open CropCare AIWhen installation finishes, tap Open.

For your safety: Only download CropCare AI from this official page.

Direct APK updates require Android confirmation. Automatic updates will become available when CropCare AI is distributed through Google Play.
